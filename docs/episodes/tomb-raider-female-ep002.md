# Tomb-Raider Female Episode 002

## Title

Mirror Tenant

## Goal

Validate continuity across real story progression with major scene changes inside the new urban-supernatural mainline.

## Narrative arc

### Shot 001

Morgue, late night.
Lin Xia finishes the autopsy and notices the victim's reflection moving before the body does.

### Shot 002

Apartment block, rainy night.
She follows the whispered address to a dim residential corridor where elevator mirrors show the dead tenant standing behind her.

### Shot 003

Evidence room / occult reveal.
She opens a sealed case file and finds the same token from the morgue attached to a missing-person photo of her younger sister.

## Continuity anchors

- same protagonist face and hairstyle
- same leather jacket, grey hoodie, black cargo pants, boots
- same child charm pendant
- escalating supernatural tone from clinical realism to overt anomaly

## Scene-change requirement

This episode must prove that continuity can survive:

- morgue to apartment transition
- apartment to evidence-room transition
- changing light sources from clinical white to sodium rain light to archive darkness
- increasing supernatural intensity without changing the protagonist's identity

# Tomb-Raider Female Episode 003

## Title

Contract Night

## Mainline

This episode abandons the old tomb-adventure setup and follows the new urban supernatural case line.

## Narrative arc

### Shot 001

Late-night municipal morgue.
Lin Xia witnesses the unidentified corpse open its eyes during autopsy.

### Shot 002

Rain-soaked apartment corridor.
She follows the whispered address to room 404 and sees the dead tenant in the elevator mirror.

### Shot 003

Hidden midnight auction.
The clue from the apartment leads her into a clandestine hall where forbidden evidence objects are being traded.

## What this episode is testing

- real story progression across three different locations
- continuity under strong lighting changes
- continuity under wardrobe stability but increasing narrative complexity
- whether per-shot keyframes perform better than one shared anchor image

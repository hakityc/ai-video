# Tomb-Raider Female Episode 001

## Title

The Lamp Beneath the Sand

## Goal

Validate short-form continuity with a shared character anchor over three consecutive video shots.

## Continuity anchors

- Same protagonist: Lin Yue
- Same outfit: brown leather jacket, grey hoodie, black cargo pants, work boots
- Same prop: flashlight in hand
- Same environment family: desert tomb interior, blue-grey low light, dust in air
- Same tone: restrained dread, grounded cinematic realism

## Shot list

### Shot 001

Lin Yue steps into the wind-carved tomb corridor, raising her flashlight into drifting dust.

### Shot 002

Closer shot. She pauses, turns slightly, and notices soot on the wall shifting like distant figures.

### Shot 003

Wider reveal. She faces a sealed chamber mural while a delayed shadow movement hints at the supernatural.

## Review checklist

- Face shape and hairstyle remain stable
- Jacket and hoodie remain stable
- Flashlight remains present and believable
- Lighting, dust, and cave palette feel continuous
- Emotional progression feels sequential rather than random

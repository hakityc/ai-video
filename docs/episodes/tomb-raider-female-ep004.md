# Tomb-Raider Female Episode 004

## Title

The Autopsy That Opened Its Eyes

## Goal

Validate continuity on a single self-contained mini-story instead of cross-chapter jumps.

## Narrative arc

### Shot 001

Late-night setup.
Lin Xia enters the morgue, reviews the case file, and prepares for autopsy.

### Shot 002

Inciting anomaly.
The corpse opens its eyes during the examination and Lin Xia freezes.

### Shot 003

Immediate consequence.
The report changes on its own, the recorder glitches, and Lin Xia realizes this is not a normal case.

## What this episode is testing

- one location
- one short continuous action chain
- low scene variance with rising tension
- whether identity holds better when the story is compressed into a single chapter beat

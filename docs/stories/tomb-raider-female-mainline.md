# Tomb-Raider Female Mainline

## Chosen source spine

The new mainline is rebuilt around the high-level premise of Fanqie novel:

- `夜小风的第1本书`

Source page:

- [番茄小说作品页](https://fanqienovel.com/page/7497069897994734654)

What we take from the source premise:

- a female forensic examiner as protagonist
- the inciting incident happens during an autopsy
- the protagonist is forced into a supernatural contract
- each case is both standalone and part of a larger conspiracy
- the season-long emotional engine is a missing younger sister

We are replacing the old tomb-adventure frame entirely.
No ruins, no expedition, no underground relic hunt.

## Why this source fits better

This structure is stronger for continuity testing because it naturally creates:

- recurring protagonist identity across sharply different locations
- story escalation through consecutive cases
- cleaner scene transitions between morgue, street, apartment, archive, and ritual spaces
- a built-in state manager around clues, contracts, and missing-person threads

## Our working adaptation

### Source-style spine

- forensic autopsy gone wrong
- supernatural contract
- special-case investigations
- hidden network behind the cases
- missing sister as long arc

### Project version

The protagonist is now `Lin Xia`, a young forensic examiner in a modern Chinese city.
During the autopsy of an unidentified woman, she witnesses a corpse opening its eyes and speaking a warning only she can hear.
That night, she is coerced into a ten-year pact with a death-adjacent bureaucracy and becomes a handler for anomalous cases that ordinary police files cannot close.

Every new case points toward the same hidden network that may also be connected to her younger sister's disappearance.

## Season engine

Each episode should uncover one more layer:

1. Lin Xia realizes the first corpse was marked, not random
2. the contract gives her access to scenes others cannot perceive
3. multiple victims are linked by the same underworld token
4. someone inside the system is steering cases toward her
5. her sister's disappearance is part of the same chain

## Episode progression

### Episode 001

- late-night autopsy of an unidentified woman
- first paranormal contact on the dissection table
- forced signing of the contract

### Episode 002

- first field assignment in a rain-soaked apartment block
- she traces a dead tenant's echo through elevators and corridor mirrors
- finds the first clue tied to her sister

### Episode 003

- enters a clandestine auction dealing in human relics and memory objects
- discovers the same seal used in the autopsy room
- confirms the conspiracy is organized, not folkloric coincidence

## Prompting implication

The story should now emphasize:

- autopsy lamps and stainless-steel clinical spaces
- wet urban night streets
- apartment corridors, elevators, archives, and evidence rooms
- underworld contract imagery
- restrained modern supernatural realism

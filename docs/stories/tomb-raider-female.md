# Tomb-Raider Female Story Concept

## Positioning

A modern Chinese urban-supernatural procedural with the atmosphere of:

- forensic realism
- wet neon night streets
- cramped apartment corridors
- bureaucratic occult systems
- emotionally cold but visually grounded suspense

It now follows a forensic and paranormal-investigation frame, not an expedition or ruins-adventure frame.

## Protagonist

Name: Lin Xia

Visual anchor:

- short ash-blonde bob
- brown leather jacket over grey hoodie
- black fitted work pants
- messenger evidence bag and nitrile gloves
- calm, focused, slightly severe expression

Core trait:

- outwardly clinical and rational
- inwardly fixated on the unresolved disappearance of her younger sister

## Story Hook

Lin Xia is a city forensic examiner assigned a late-night autopsy on an unidentified female victim.
Under the operating light, the corpse briefly revives and whispers a warning about a debt that has not been collected.

Hours later, Lin Xia is approached by entities posing as civil servants from a hidden death-management bureau.
She is forced into a contract that binds her to unresolved supernatural cases that intersect with ordinary police work.

## Season-Long Engine

Each case seems isolated at first, but all of them carry the same signature token and point toward the same hidden trafficking network.
The season engine is not treasure or excavation.
It is the convergence of:

- anomalous deaths
- illicit relic and memory trading
- a hidden bureaucracy managing the dead
- the disappearance of Lin Xia's younger sister

## Pilot Episode

### Episode title

The Autopsy That Opened Its Eyes

### Opening sequence

Near midnight, Lin Xia performs an autopsy in a nearly empty municipal morgue.
The room is clinically clean, but the temperature drops, condensation gathers on stainless steel trays, and the victim's chart updates itself with a cause of death no one typed.

### First supernatural beat

As Lin Xia leans in to examine the body, the victim opens her eyes and mouths a location.
The surveillance camera records nothing unusual.
Only reflective surfaces in the morgue show a second figure standing behind Lin Xia.

### End-of-episode image

Lin Xia stands alone in a rain-dark apartment corridor, staring at a mirror where her own reflection is delayed by one second and a child's charm hangs from the doorknob of the room she is about to enter.
